FARM HEIST

Grow it. Ship it. Guard it.

The design map for a Roblox farming game. You grow crops on your own plot, pack them in your warehouse, and truck them to the city at the global price or sell them in your own shop at yours. Leave your ripe crops alone too long and someone might sneak in. Stay home and you can bonk them with your bat and get them arrested.

  • 8 farms per server
  • 22 crops · 6 rarities
  • 9 kinds of worker
  • 5 s knockout
  • offline growth 25%
  • 12 land permits by level
How the game fits together

Where crops and coins go

Crops go round clockwise: seeds into the field, the harvest into the barn, through the warehouse, out by truck or through your shop, and back as coins to spend. The red branch underneath is what happens when someone raids a field.

Step by step

The first hour, a raid, and the race

New players get a guided start and 45 minutes when nobody can rob them. After that, every raid follows the same states, and whether it works comes down to a few seconds.

Cloverdale

What the world looks like

A round green valley: the town in the middle, a ring road around it, eight farms facing the road, and hills all round with a tunnel to the City in the north.

In town

  • Barley's SeedsThe seed shop. New stock every 5 minutes, the same on every server.
  • Auction House3 lots every 8 minutes. You have to be in the hall to bid.
  • Police StationArrested players appear on the porch. Most Wanted and Sheriff boards, the mugshot wall, the debt desk.
  • Cloverdale ExchangeA grain elevator with a giant flip-board of crop prices.
  • Hiring HallPatty's job board. New worker candidates every 10 minutes.
  • Trade PlazaThe fountain and picnic tables, where people meet to trade.

Walking times

Town spawn → a farm gate~15 s Police porch → back of a farm24–36 s Home button (from anywhere)3 s

Look and feel

  • Bright low-poly and flat-shaded, like Tide Market's plant pack: chunky fences, round trees, crops 2–3× real size so you can read a field from the road.
  • A 24-minute day: warm afternoon light, an orange dusk for Golden Hour, a blue night with lantern pools and floodlights, a huge orange moon for the Harvest Moon.
  • Each farm grows its own kind of flower along the hedges, so the farms are easy to tell apart.
  • Ripe crops bounce and sparkle in their rarity colour. Mythics glow and can be seen from the ring road.
